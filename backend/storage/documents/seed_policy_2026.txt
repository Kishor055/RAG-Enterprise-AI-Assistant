ENTERPRISE REMOTE WORK & SECURITY POLICY 2026
Section 1: General Guidelines & Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for hybrid remote work up to 3 days per week upon manager approval. Remote work must be conducted using company-issued laptops equipped with full disk encryption.

Section 2: Data Confidentiality & Access Control
Employees must never store enterprise customer data on personal devices or unsecured USB drives. Access to confidential financial reports is strictly restricted to authorized role-based personnel (Finance Managers & Admins).

Section 3: Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy
Business travel expenses including flights, hotel lodging up to $250/night, and meals up to $75/day must be submitted within 14 days of completion. Receipts are mandatory for all transactions exceeding $25.

Section 4: Incident Response & Security Breaches
In case of a lost laptop or suspicious phishing email, employees must notify the Security Operations Center (SOC) within 1 hour.
